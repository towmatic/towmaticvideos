2. **Rate regulations.** Some cities, counties and states regulate towing rates, including non-consent, police

3. **Honoring quotes.** Quotes are sent on your behalf. Towmatic is not responsible for pricing errors that result
   from your rates, rules or settings, or for any dispute between you and your customer about a quote.
4. **Dispatch rules.** Jobs are routed according to the dispatch rules, rotation and truck settings you choose.
   You are responsible for the drivers and equipment that respond to each job.

## X. Text messages

1. Towmatic sends text messages on your behalf, such as quotes, booking confirmations, booking links and driver
   notifications. These messages are about a service the recipient requested.
2. You will not use Towmatic to send marketing messages without the consent the law requires, and you are
   responsible for honoring opt-out requests (such as STOP) from your customers.

signage, notice, photo or reporting requirement that applies to private-property, impound or police tows where
you operate. You are responsible for meeting those requirements.

   website are estimates. Your bill is based on your actual usage.
3. **Timelines.** App launch (typically 2–3 weeks) depends on Apple and Google review. Website launch (7 days)
