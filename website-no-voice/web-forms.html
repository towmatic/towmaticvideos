FAQ

Instant Dispatch Forms questions.

I already have a “request a tow” form on my website. Why would I need this?

Ask yourself two questions: Is someone watching it 24/7? And does it send the job straight to a driver?

Most website forms just send an email to an inbox. The request sits there until someone checks it, and by then the customer has already called the next tow company.

An Instant Dispatch Form quotes the customer from your rate sheet, gets their exact location and dispatches your next driver in seconds, any time of day, with nobody watching it. If your form doesn’t do that, it’s costing you tows and service calls.

How does the customer get a quote?

SmartQuote prices the job from your real rate sheet, and the quote is texted to the customer automatically. Your driver is dispatched only after the customer replies YES.

How does SmartLocate work?

Customers share their exact GPS location from the form in one tap, so your driver knows exactly where to go.

Can you add the form to my website?

Yes, for free. We can add a “Book a Tow” button that opens your form, or build the whole form right into your page. Your choice. Just send us your website login or put us in touch with your web person.

Where can I put the form?

On your website, on QR codes on your trucks and business cards, and at partner locations.

Which driver gets the job?

SmartDispatch sends a push notification to your next driver on call, who confirms in the Towmatic app. If they don’t confirm in time, the job rolls to the next driver.

Is there a limit on bookings?

No. Every plan includes unlimited bookings, with no caps or tiers. The texts each booking sends are covered by your $30 monthly text credit first.
