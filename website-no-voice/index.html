FAQ

Questions tow owners ask.

What if a customer needs to cancel a tow?

No problem. Every booking confirmation text includes a link the customer can use to update or cancel their tow.

What if I only want Towmatic for certain jobs (like police or private property)?

No problem! You choose which booking forms to use (ASAP, scheduled, motor club, police or private property) and where to share them. The choice is yours how you want to use it.

Can I see every booking Towmatic takes?

Yes! You’ll have access to your own Towmatic dashboard where you can see every booking, tracked to where it came from.

Can I change my rates?

Yes, any time, from your dashboard. Quotes use your rates from that point on.

Does it know my service area?

Yes. You set the states you work in and it turns down work outside them.

Can I change things myself, or do I have to call you?

Almost everything is yours to change: your rates, your rotation, your booking forms, your properties and agencies. We’re here if you want help, but you’re not waiting on us.
