FAQ

Questions tow owners ask.

What if a customer calls to cancel a tow?

No problem. When the customer calls back, they will be asked for their information. Towmatic will find the tow and notify the driver that the call was cancelled.

What if I only want Towmatic for certain calls (like police or private property)?

No problem! Towmatic can be customized to only answer certain call types. The choice is yours how you want to use it.

Can I see how many calls Towmatic has answered? Can I listen back to the phone calls?

Yes! You’ll have access to your own Towmatic dashboard where you can see call logs, transcripts, and listen to call recordings for every call.
