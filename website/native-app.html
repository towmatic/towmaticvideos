FAQ

Branded app questions.

How long until my app is live?

Allow 1–2 weeks for App Store and Google Play review.

Whose name is on the app?

Yours. The app carries your company’s name and logo.

Who is the app for?

Dealers, body shops, property managers and repeat cash customers: anyone who books with you often.

What happens when someone books in the app?

The job is dispatched automatically to your next driver on call. The driver confirms in the Towmatic app, and if they don’t confirm in time, it rolls to the next driver.

Do my drivers need an app?

Every plan includes the Towmatic app for drivers. It delivers a push notification for each job, and the driver confirms in the app. Drivers who aren’t signed in get a text or phone call instead.
