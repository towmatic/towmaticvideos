FAQ

Instant Dispatch Forms questions.

How fast can I go live?

The same day you sign up.

How does the customer get a quote?

SmartQuote prices the job from your real rate sheet, and the quote is texted to the customer automatically. Your driver is dispatched only after the customer replies YES.

How does SmartLocate work?

Customers share their exact GPS location from the form in one tap, so your driver knows exactly where to go.

Where can I put the form?

On your website, on QR codes on your trucks and business cards, and at partner locations.

Which driver gets the job?

SmartDispatch sends a push notification to your next driver on call, who confirms in the Towmatic app. If they don’t confirm in time, the job rolls to the next driver.
