FAQ

Voice AI questions.

How does Voice AI price a tow?

SmartQuote prices each call from your real rate sheet: mileage, fees, vehicle class and time of day. The quote is texted to the customer automatically. Your driver gets nothing until the customer replies YES.

Does it speak Spanish?

Yes. Voice AI is multilingual, including Spanish.

What happens after a job is booked?

SmartDispatch sends a push notification to your next driver on call. The driver confirms in the Towmatic app. If they don’t confirm in time, the job rolls to the next driver.

Can a caller reach a real person?

Yes. Transfer to Human is one of the built-in call types.

What does Voice AI cost?

Plans are month-to-month with no contracts. Voice AI minutes are billed at cost, typically $0.15–$0.25 per minute. See pricing.
