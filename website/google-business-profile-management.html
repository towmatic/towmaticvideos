Review replies

FAQ

Towmatic Local questions.

What does Towmatic Local cost?

$197 a month on top of any Towmatic plan, month-to-month. No setup fee and no other charges. It’s already included in Towmatic Max. See pricing.

Do I need another Towmatic plan?

Yes. Towmatic Local is an add-on to any Towmatic plan, and it’s already included in Towmatic Max. That’s how every post can link to your Instant Dispatch Form, so customers book right from your profile.

Do I have to do anything?

Just add Towmatic as a manager on your Google Business Profile. We handle the posts and review replies from there.

How often do you post?

Once or twice a week, every week, and every post includes a link to book a tow.

Can I approve review replies before they post?

Yes. Turn on approvals and you’ll see each reply before it goes live, or let us reply to every review for you.

What do you say to a bad review?

We reply calmly and professionally, thank the customer for the feedback and invite them to reach out so you can make it right. We never argue with a customer in public.

Will this guarantee I rank #1 on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee a ranking, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Google ranks on relevance, distance and prominence. We keep your profile active and your reviews answered, which are the parts you can control.
