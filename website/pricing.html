FAQ

Pricing questions.

How does usage billing work?

Voice AI usage is billed per minute based on actual call duration, typically ranging from $0.15–$0.25 per minute depending on call complexity. Every subscription—whether you choose Voice AI, Web Forms, or bundle both—includes $30/month in communication credits. These credits cover driver dispatch notifications (calls, texts, and emails) and customer confirmations. That’s approximately 500-600 dispatches per month included at no extra cost.
