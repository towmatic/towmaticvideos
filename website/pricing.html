FAQ

Pricing questions.

How does usage billing work?

Voice AI usage is billed per minute based on actual call duration, approximately $0.20 per minute, a little more or less depending on the call. Every subscription—whether you choose Voice AI, Web Forms, or bundle both—includes $30/month in communication credits. These credits cover text messages, like quotes, booking confirmations and driver dispatch texts. That’s approximately 500-600 dispatches per month included at no extra cost.
