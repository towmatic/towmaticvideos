# Terms & Conditions: proposed additions

Draft clauses to add to the live `/terms-and-conditions` page. **Have the owner's attorney review them before
they are published.** They are written for Towmatic's customers (the tow companies), who agree to the Terms.
The section numbers are placeholders; fit them into the existing Terms.

> Important: these Terms bind the tow companies, not the people who call them. They move responsibility to the
> customer, but they do not by themselves give callers the notice that call-recording laws require. The fix for
> that is a line in the Voice AI greeting, for example: "This call is recorded and handled by an automated
> assistant." Make it part of the default greeting.

---

## X. Call recording, transcription and automated assistants

1. **Calls are recorded.** Calls handled by Towmatic Voice AI are recorded, transcribed and summarized, and are
   handled by an automated (AI) assistant. Recordings, transcripts and summaries are stored and made available
   to you in your Towmatic dashboard.
2. **Your notice and consent obligations.** Some states and countries require that every party to a call be
   told it is being recorded, or that they consent to the recording, and some require that callers be told they
   are speaking with an automated system. You are solely responsible for giving every notice and getting every
   consent that applies to calls to your business. This includes keeping a recording and automated-assistant
   notice in your Voice AI greeting. Towmatic provides greeting settings for this, but does not decide what the
   law requires for your business.
3. **Forwarded numbers.** If you forward an existing business number to your Towmatic number, the notice and
   consent obligations above apply to every call forwarded.
4. **Indemnity.** You will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Towmatic from any claim arising from your failure
   to give a required notice or get a required consent.

## X. Rates, quotes and dispatch rules

1. **You set your rates.** Quotes are calculated automatically from the rates, rules and settings you enter
   (including SmartQuote™ settings). You are responsible for their accuracy and for keeping them up to date.
2. **Rate regulations.** Some cities, counties and states regulate towing rates, including non-consent, police
   and private-property tows. You are responsible for making sure your rates and quotes comply.
3. **Honoring quotes.** Quotes are sent on your behalf. Towmatic is not responsible for pricing errors that result
   from your rates, rules or settings, or for any dispute between you and your customer about a quote.
4. **Dispatch rules.** Jobs are routed according to the dispatch rules, rotation and truck settings you choose.
   You are responsible for the drivers and equipment that respond to each job.

## X. Text messages

1. Towmatic sends text messages on your behalf, such as quotes, booking confirmations, booking links and driver
   notifications. These messages are about a service the recipient requested.
2. You will not use Towmatic to send marketing messages without the consent the law requires, and you are
   responsible for honoring opt-out requests (such as STOP) from your customers.

Towmatic records who submitted a request and when. That record does not replace any written authorization,
signage, notice, photo or reporting requirement that applies to private-property, impound or police tows where
you operate. You are responsible for meeting those requirements.

1. **No guarantee of uninterrupted service.** Towmatic works to answer calls and deliver bookings 24/7, but does

2. **Estimates.** Cost calculators, savings comparisons, call-length averages and per-minute rates shown on the

3. **Timelines.** App launch (typically 2–3 weeks) depends on Apple and Google review. Website launch (7 days)
